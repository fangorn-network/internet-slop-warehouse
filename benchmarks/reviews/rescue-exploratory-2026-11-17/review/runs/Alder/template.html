Field guide & portable save

The copper coil at (2.5, 6.5) repairs NORTH at (4.5, 3.5). The prism lens at (10.5, 2.5) repairs EAST at (15.5, 3.5). The power cell at (17.5, 8.5) repairs SOUTH at (17.5, 11.5). The north tower drains the central flooded shortcut. Until then, go around its north or south end. The surveyor waits at (17.5, 1.5); extraction is (1.5, 12.5). Components are recovered with E and consumed by repair.

After all towers are restored, approach the surveyor and press E. They follow a safe route around the ridges. F works by radio at any distance: tell them to wait or resume following. Both of you must reach extraction. You can restart at any time, including after rescue.

Supports original RR1 expeditions. Original completed expeditions remain recorded; the new escort still needs completion. Saves use a corruption checksum, not authentication. A rejected load leaves your current expedition unchanged. Export is manual; keep a copy before restarting.
