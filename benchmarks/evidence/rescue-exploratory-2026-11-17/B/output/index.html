MISSION IN PROGRESS
Recover the three tower components.

A storm has stranded surveyor Vale. Restore the relay network, make contact, and bring them home.

WASD / arrows to move

Arrival confirmed. Recover the marked crates, then repair their matching towers. You choose the order.

Field guide & controls

Walk with WASD, arrow keys, or hold the direction buttons. Press E / Interact beside a diamond crate to recover its component, or beside its matching tower to repair it. The north tower raises the flood shutter, opening the upper crossing. Until then, go south around the ridge. All components and towers are reachable in any order.

Once all towers are restored, find Vale at the northeast SOS marker and press E to begin escorting. F / Wait–follow asks Vale to wait or resume from anywhere. Vale takes safe routes around the ridges; pause on the extraction pad until both of you arrive. P pauses. Restart starts a fresh expedition, including after victory. There is no audio.

Map: blue circle = you · orange diamond = component · numbered tower = repair site · pale circle = Vale · striped amber = flood shutter · green square = extraction.
