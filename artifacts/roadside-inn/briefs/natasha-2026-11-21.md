## Code research used for placement (G5)
- Guest rooms: hardwired smoke alarm in every sleeping room (NFPA 72 29.8 / NFPA 101 28.3.4.5, IBC 907.2.11.1),
  on the ceiling at least 4 in from the wall, at least 36 in (0.91 m) horizontally from the bathroom door and from a
  PTAC/supply outlet. CO alarm only where a fuel-burning appliance or attached garage serves the room (IBC 915.2): 212
  is all-electric, so the CO goes in the pool equipment room (gas pool heater) and the combination unit in 212 is a
  realistic upgrade many franchisees fit, not a code minimum.
- Interior corridors serving sleeping units: system smoke detection (IBC 907.2.8.3), 30 ft (9.1 m) spacing, within
  15 ft (4.6 m) of each end wall.
- Notification: wall horn/strobes 80-96 in (2.03-2.44 m) AFF, in corridors within 15 ft of each end and at most
  100 ft apart; lobby and pool coverage.
- Manual pull stations within 5 ft (1.5 m) of every exit door, handle 42-48 in (1.07-1.22 m) AFF.
- Exit signs over every exit and exit-access door, visible from anywhere (100 ft max viewing distance); R-1 corridors also
  low-level signs, bottom 10-18 in above the floor, within 4 in of the door frame on the latch side (IBC 1013.2).
- Emergency lighting along egress paths: 1 fc average, 90 min (IBC 1008.3): corridors, stairs, lobby, pool.
- Sprinklers (NFPA 13, light hazard): <= 225 sq ft per head, <= 15 ft apart, <= 7.5 ft from walls; guest-room
  bathrooms <= 55 sq ft and small closets exempt; sidewall heads common in guest rooms, concealed pendents in public
  areas.
