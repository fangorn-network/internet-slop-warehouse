# Cast, 2026-11-21: gait and pose (L1, L2; notes on E1 and R7)

## What the study found (projects/roadside-inn/GAIT-AND-POSE.md)
Real people at rest stay still. That stillness is broken by short, deliberate events: a gaze shift to something, a
weight shift onto the other leg, a change of seated posture when what they're doing changes. Between events the only
motion is breathing, millimetres of sway and blinks. Our residents were the reverse: everything moved a little all the
time for no reason, and nothing they looked at was a real thing. Root causes:
1. A standing idle clip (hips 6-11 deg) played under the seated pose. This is the sofa guy's "wily shifting".
2. A sine-wave weight shift on top of a looping idle. The idle's own head also looked around by itself (+-25-35 deg).
3. No gaze targets, no eyes, and a turret-style look-at.
4. Slow walkers in slow motion: the clip was time-scaled, so the stride stayed the same and only the cadence dropped.

The study turns these into numbers, with sources: walk ratio, fixation and look durations (TV attentional inertia),
eye-head coordination, civil inattention, Duarte and Zatsiorsky weight shifts, in-chair movement rates, breathing and
sway. Section 9 has the before/after measurements.

## What changed
- `body.js` (new, CC0): the study as data (`STUDY`) plus logic, with no rig and no renderer. It provides attention
  (one fixation at a time on a task, a person, a noise, or "away"), the eye/head/torso split, held stances and shifts,
  breathing, sway, blinks and walk cadence. `body.test.js` has 7 seeded tests.
- `people.js`:
  - Standing people hold one of two contrapposto frames from the authored idle (hips over one foot, then the other) and
    shift weight now and then (~1 s minimum-jerk; holds median 22 s). Neck and head are held facing straight ahead.
  - Seated people use a frozen base pose plus breathing. A posture change blends over 2 s.
  - Gaze: the eyes jump first, the head follows over about 0.3 s, a sound turns the head first, and the eyes re-centre
    as the head arrives.
  - Walk: cadence follows sqrt(v) (constant walk ratio). Leg and arm swing scale with step length, and the lowest foot
    is re-grounded.
  - Arm IK now runs after the gaze. On these rigs the collarbones hang off the neck, so head turns had been pulling the
    hands off their targets.
  - The lead's bind-pose reset is kept. All procedural motion is per frame and nothing accumulates.
- `intent.js`: new steps `['posture', name]` and `['attend', thing]`, and a seated-resident schema (seat, postures,
  things, manner). Test added.
- `world/staff.js`:
  - Sitters are now intent residents. Gene alternates watching the TV with checking his phone, and the phone is a real
    prop that follows his hands.
  - The nurse attends to her phone and the rep to his screen. Dana attends to her monitor and engages a guest at 3.2 m.
  - Walkers look at the path ahead, so the head leads the turns, and at what they're doing when they stop.
  - New `game.hear(pos, joules)`.
  - The nurse's feet are on the stool's measured front rail (top 0.46 m at z -4.78; there is no ring at 0.26). Her
    hands sit just over the table's near edge (z -4.95), within reach.
- `check.mjs people`: new Cast assertions (stillness, no seated rocking, attention on task, Gene looks at you when you
  stand near, noise orienting with the eyes leading, smooth posture change, contrapposto stances, slow walk without
  skating, bone drift over 20 s). Two existing assertions changed:
  - The rate assertion now expects the walk-ratio law.
  - The breathing out-of-step window went from 3 s to 30 s: the 3 s window failed 2.7 % of the time by chance.
- `REALISM.md`: new "## Cast (2026-11-21)" section.
