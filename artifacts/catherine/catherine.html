Sleep and Dream Database · melvin-dreams · 10 January 1993

Catherine

In this hallway there was a ghost. Very clearly seen. White, slightly translucent. I heard the sound of walking heavily on a wooden floor, clomp, clomp. The ghost was walking several feet off the ground at a level where there had been a floor in the past… The ghost’s name was Catherine… I reached out to touch the ghost, and my hand passed through it with only the faintest sensation of resistance… She was aware of me, and seemed to be bending to peer down, but I never saw her face. She reached down to touch me, and her hand passed through mine.
