New expedition & location

Peaceful by default. A new expedition applies this setting and starts a fresh camp. Drawing the carbine is optional; no bots attack you.

Choosing another district starts a new camp. Save your current expedition first.

Field controls

Left stick walks; push farther to run. Drag the world to look; on desktop, hold the right mouse button. Explore offers street-following routes and a pinch/pan atlas. Camp opens blueprints; place on open ground. Pack holds gear, rest and return-to-camp. Desktop: WASD, Shift, E interact, C camp.
