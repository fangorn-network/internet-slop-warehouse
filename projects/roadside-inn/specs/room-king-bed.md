## Read of the place (≤ 120 words)
Lights on: warm 2700-3000 K from two swing-arm sconces on the headboard panel, a desk lamp and a floor lamp; no ceiling fixture in any photo, so the room is a set of pools and the ceiling and corners stay dim. Lights off, drapes closed, night: very dark but not black; blackout liner leaves 1-3 cm bright slits at the centre overlap and side edges (cool sodium/LED yellow-white from the lot), a faint strip under the entry door from the corridor (~3-8 lux, 2 cm tall, cool white), and tiny always-on LEDs (PTAC display, smoke detector, TV standby, clock/phone). Constant PTAC compressor hum and fan cycling, door-closer thunk, corridor and ice-machine noise. Smell of carpet cleaner and fabric softener. Used look: scuffed baseboards, dented wall by the bed, carpet worn on the walking line.
