## Read of the place (≤ 120 words)
Light: a large backlit mirror whose whole border glows cool white (~4000 K), plus a round ceiling flush disc with a blue-lit ring, so the room reads cold and even, no warm vanity bar; the tub end is dimmer. Lights off, door
open: dim spill from the bedroom only; door closed, near black except a thin line under the door. Sound: fan drone
(~45 dB) when switched on with the light; drips from the tub spout. Smell: bleach/cleaner, damp towel. Look: tile and
white fixtures clean but the caulk and grout are yellowed at the tub, the chrome has water spots, towels thin and grey-white.
A guest first sees the tub with its curtain, the towel stack, and the vanity mirror.

## Lighting
- Backlit mirror, ~4000 K edge glow: lights the face level and counter; roughly half of the light (photos 1,3).
- Ceiling round LED disc with a blue-lit ring, 4000 K: pool on the tub and toilet; switched with the mirror.
- Night: no night-light assumed. Door shut, both off: black except a thin line under the door (~91 cm wide) from the bedroom
  lamp or hall. Door open: dim wedge from the bedroom.
- Daylight: none; no window.

## Not here
- No window and no daylight in most king baths of this class.
- No walk-in shower in a standard king here (tub/shower combo, photos 1,2); no grab bars except an accessible room.
- No bidet, no radiator, no scale, no phone, no heated towel rail, no robe, no over-toilet shelf, no cabinet doors under the vanity, no vanity light bar, no separate wall tile.
- No floor drain; no carpet; no bath bomb or amenity basket beyond the tray.
- No smoke detector in the bath (often one in the bedroom, outside).
