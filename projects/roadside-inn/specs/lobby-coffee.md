## Read of the place (≤ 120 words)
Lit by recessed LED cans overhead (3500-4000 K), no under-counter strip; the charcoal backer panel soaks light so the glossy white top and the blue towers are what you see. Counter, left to right: wood cup rack, two COFFEE towers, a DECAF tower, a hot-water box, a wood riser of bins, a wicker basket of creamers, a stir-straw tumbler, a hole in the top for trash. No brewer in view; smell is stale roast and warm plastic lids. Sugar grains, brown rings and drips cover the tray; the lid bins are half empty and mixed by size. Guests notice the blue towers first, then that the tap lever is sticky.
