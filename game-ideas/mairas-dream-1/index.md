The dream opens: you walk into a well lit thrift store. Think Goodwill. 
The shelves are fully stocked, racks and shelves of varied and interesting items can be found throughout the store. There are other customers buying things, and the cash register is actually functional and staffed. You have $30 USD to purcahse items. Maybe a mechanic could be a 'tag switch' where you try to switch a tag to make something cheaper, or maybe you're trying to steal something? I think that each journey through the game should spawn a randomized objective (e.g. get materials do some specific task like... break into your car because you lockecd your keys in so you need a clothes hanger only, or maybe you need to build a shed so you have to find hardware and a hammer and things). It should generally be pretty tongue in cheek. At the back of the store is the manager, a dark gray man in a clow costume who is always monitoring the store. If he catches you, you get taken to the managers office, which is effectively game-over (in this version - maybe it can be expanded later on). 

After making purchases, you have to leave the store. There are four cats that start running around lighing little fires with matches. Black catrs. The doors are automatic (i.e. we need to mimic a motion sensor to open the sliding doors as an effect). In the parking lot, your car is at the far end, and cats have follow you outside and started setting fire to everything around - so you need to make it out before they torch your car and path out. 

Context on Goodwill/store layout: the registers are at the front, nearest the doors. the doors are automatic and have a motion sensor. there must be a fire extinguisher available - we should actually look into building codes/compliance as part of this. This information could additionally be published to fangorn either in this repo or a new one for our own needs later. The store is then sections by gender and categeory: mens/womens/children first for clothing and shoes, though it's a single shoe section with all-gender shoes. Each male/female/children section is some collection of racks, both linear and circular, arranged to showcase tops (short sleeve, long sleeve, casual, formal, all separated) and bottoms (similarly separated). The store also sells undergarments and socks. There is usually a furniture section (think used furniture), electronics (e.g. old lamps, used coffee makers,  usually trashy but sometimes some cool retro pieces). Then there's a home/kitchen section with plates/cups/mugs/utensils/decorations/etc, then books/dvds/media, and then mayber a small candy area with knick knacks, sunglasses, that sort of thing.
